Food Standards in Bangladesh

Food standards ensure that food is safe, healthy and of good quality. In Bangladesh, the two main organizations responsible for food safety and quality are BFSA and BSTI.

Bangladesh Food Safety Authority (BFSA)

  • Ensures safe food for consumers.
  • Inspects restaurants, markets and food factories.
  • Monitors food production and distribution.
  • Takes legal action against unsafe food.

Bangladesh Standards and Testing Institution (BSTI)

  • Sets quality standards for food products.
  • Tests food samples in laboratories.
  • Provides BSTI certification.
  • Controls the quality of packaged food.

Storage Conditions for Different Foods

Food Item Temperature Moisture Freshness Range
Milk 0°C - 4°C High 2 - 5 days
Fish 0°C - 2°C High 1 - 2 days
Meat 0°C - 4°C High 3 - 5 days
Eggs 4°C - 7°C Medium 3 - 5 weeks
Rice Room Temperature Low 6 - 12 months
Potato 7°C - 10°C Low 1 - 2 months
Apple 0°C - 4°C Medium 1 - 2 months
Banana 13°C - 15°C Medium 5 - 7 days

Note: Proper temperature and moisture help maintain food quality and freshness for a longer period.
