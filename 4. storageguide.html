Proper food storage helps keep food fresh, safe and nutritious. Storing food at the correct temperature and moisture level reduces spoilage and prevents the growth of harmful microorganisms.

General Storage Tips

  • Keep refrigerated foods in the refrigerator.
  • Store dry foods in a cool and dry place.
  • Always check the expiry date before use.
  • Keep raw meat and fish separate from cooked food.
  • Use clean and airtight containers whenever possible.

Storage Guide for Common Foods

Food Storage Temperature Moisture Freshness Storage Place
Milk 0°C - 4°C High 2 - 5 days Refrigerator
Meat 0°C - 4°C High 3 - 5 days Refrigerator
Fish 0°C - 2°C High 1 - 2 days Refrigerator
Egg 4°C - 7°C Medium 3 - 5 weeks Refrigerator
Potato 7°C - 10°C Low 1 - 2 months Cool, Dry Place

Important Tips

  1. Do not keep milk outside for a long time.
  2. Cook meat and fish before the freshness period ends.
  3. Do not wash eggs before storing them.
  4. Store potatoes away from direct sunlight.
  5. Throw away food with a bad smell or unusual color.
